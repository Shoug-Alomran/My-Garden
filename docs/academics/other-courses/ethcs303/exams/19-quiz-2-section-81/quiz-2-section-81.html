Q1
How is Cloud Computing defined?
✓Correct Answer: b)
Cloud computing is defined by scalable IT capability delivered “as a service” over Internet technologies — the defining traits are scale and service delivery, not local hardware (a) or network restriction (c).
Q2
What is the primary driver behind the feasibility and affordability of Equipment as a Service (EaaS)?
✓Correct Answer: a)
EaaS became practical because IoT connectivity lets vendors monitor, meter and service equipment remotely, so equipment can be sold as an ongoing service rather than a one-off purchase.
Q3
How do automatic software updates in the cloud benefit an organization's IT department?
✓Correct Answer: c)
Suppliers refresh the software centrally, so the organization saves IT staff time and outside consultation costs. Cloud delivery still requires connectivity (a), and updates are automatic rather than manual (b).
Q4
How does a cloud-based system maintain “Quality Control” in reporting?
✓Correct Answer: c)
Quality control comes from a single store and single format — one version of the truth, consistent processes, and an audit trail of revisions, instead of divergent local copies.
Q5
Why does the cloud offer “Loss prevention” compared to regular localized servers?
✓Correct Answer: b)
Loss prevention is about data surviving the device: if the local machine is lost, stolen or broken, the data is still reachable from any connected computer. It is not a claim about passwords (a) or encryption (c).
Q6
If attackers gain access to a user's credentials via account hijacking, what critical areas of cloud computing services can they compromise?
✓Correct Answer: b)
Stolen credentials put the whole CIA triad at risk — the attacker can read data (confidentiality), alter it (integrity) and disrupt or delete the service (availability).
Q7
What legal complication arises if cloud data is stored in a data center located in a different country from where the customer lives?
✓Correct Answer: a)
Data is governed by the laws of the jurisdiction it sits in, so cross-border storage can place the customer under foreign law and out of compliance with their own local regulations. Hosting does not transfer IP ownership (b).
Q8
How did local program security prior to cloud computing protect user privacy from vendors?
✓Correct Answer: c)
When both the program and its data lived on the user's own machine, the vendor had no channel to the data or to usage telemetry. Moving the program to the vendor's servers is what created that exposure.
Q9
In data mining, what is the underlying danger of utilizing Big Data profiling to sort citizens?
✓Correct Answer: d)
Profiling turns combinations of attributes into categories, and those categories can then be used for discrimination or targeted denial of service — pricing, insurance, hiring or credit decisions made about a person because of the group the model puts them in.
Q10
Why is a compromise of biometric data considered “catastrophic” compared to losing a credit card or Social Security Number?
✓Correct Answer: d)
A card or ID number can be cancelled and replaced; a fingerprint or iris cannot be revoked or reissued. Once the template leaks, the identifier is compromised permanently.
